The rules

How to win at court

Each player starts with 2 coins and 2 hidden influence cards (3 copies of each character in the deck). Lose both and you’re out. Last one standing wins.

  1. Income — take 1 coin. Can’t be blocked.
  2. Foreign Aid — take 2 coins. Any player may block as the Duke.
  3. Coup — pay 7 coins; a rival loses an influence. Can’t be blocked. Mandatory at 10+ coins.
  4. Tax (Duke) — take 3 coins.
  5. Assassinate (Assassin) — pay 3 coins; a rival loses an influence. The target may block as the Contessa.
  6. Steal (Captain) — take 2 coins from a rival. The target may block as the Captain or Ambassador.
  7. Exchange (Ambassador) — draw 2 cards, keep as many as you hold, return the rest.

Challenges: any character claim, action or block, can be challenged. If the claimer shows the card, the challenger loses an influence and the claimer swaps that card for a new one. If not, the claimer loses an influence and the action fails.
